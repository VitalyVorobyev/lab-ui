`ContourEditor` takes `bounds` (image-coordinate clamp area; default unchanged). `DraftShape` takes `stroke` (CSS colour override), a `closing` cue on polygons, and new `stroke` (brush trail) and `brush` (footprint cursor) kinds. `MaskEditor` draws its brush ring with the `brush` footprint and shows it under a hovering pointer.
